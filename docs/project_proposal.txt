Project Proposal
Smart Living Ecosystem for Bangladesh
(Mess, Hostel, Hotel & Apartment Management Platform)

1. Vision & Problem Statement
Current Problems in Bangladesh
Students & job holders struggle to find mess/hostel with verified info
Newcomers to Dhaka, Chattogram, Sylhet face scams & misinformation
Apartment owners manage renters using paper, Excel, or WhatsApp
Renters have no transparent billing, reminders, or complaint tracking
No single unified platform for:
Finding accommodation
Booking
Managing rent, bills, notices
Owner–tenant communication

Vision
Build Bangladesh’s first unified smart living platform that connects seekers, renters, owners, mess/hostel managers, and hotels in one modern application.

2. Target Users
